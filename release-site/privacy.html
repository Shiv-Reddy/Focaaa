How Focaaa handles your information

Focaaa is a focus timer and task list with an optional private conversation. Both people must choose to pair and verify a comparison code. This notice describes the current direct APK release.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, PIN, contact names, message history, and conversation keys are stored locally. Focaaa uses Android Keystore and an encrypted local database to protect saved private conversations. Sent and downloaded photo or video files are kept as ciphertext in app-private storage. It does not automatically upload your message history or tasks. A history backup is created only when you choose to save one; the encrypted file can be opened with its separate recovery key. The backup includes message records but not photo or video files. Restored history is read only and cannot restore an active pair or missing media.

What the online services handle

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account identifier for this app installation. Firebase App Check checks app requests. Firebase Cloud Messaging supplies a device push token so the relay can send content-free delivery hints. Cloudflare Workers and D1 hold pairing records, device and anonymous account identifiers, push tokens, rate-limit records, encrypted message envelopes awaiting delivery, encrypted media chunks, and message delivery and read timestamps linked to envelope IDs. The relay does not receive message plaintext, media keys, file names, file types, local contact names, or your PIN. Push hints do not contain message text.

For each selected photo or video, Focaaa encrypts the bytes on the sending phone. The encrypted copy is uploaded to the relay in chunks. Its decryption key and file type travel only inside the existing encrypted conversation message. Photos are limited to 2 MiB and short MP4 videos to 10 MiB in this release. The receiving phone downloads and checks ciphertext before showing it in the private area.

Live draft sharing and the invite roles

In version 1.8.2, the person joining an invite must check an initially unchecked live-draft box before joining. It explains that the invite creator can see changes to the joiner's message box before a message is sent, including text the joiner deletes or replaces. The box is required for this version's new invite flow; you can decline it and continue using Focaaa's focus timer and tasks without joining. There is no separate permission popup. After the joiner checks the box and pairing completes, sharing starts when that person opens the unlocked private chat. A visible control in chat lets the joiner pause, resume, or turn sharing off. Existing pairs do not start sharing on upgrade; the joiner must explicitly enable it in chat.

The invite creator can view the joiner's live draft and recent edit history. The joiner cannot view the creator's draft or edit history, and the creator's message-box edits are not sent through the live-draft service. Ordinary sent messages still work both ways. The app records message-box text changes in order, with timestamps, inserted and removed text, and a session sequence. Pasting and keyboard corrections can produce one text change; this is a record of composer changes, not a guaranteed log of physical keys. No PIN, invite code, password field, task, or other input is monitored by this feature.

Phones encrypt each draft edit with a key derived from their verified conversation and send ciphertext over an authenticated WebSocket. The Cloudflare relay checks the registered pair, device, recorded joiner choice, and frame direction; it forwards only joiner edits to the creator and acknowledgements or replay requests back to the joiner. The relay sees pair and device identifiers, timing, sequence, and ciphertext size, but not draft text. Firebase Authentication and App Check authenticate requests. Draft text is not put in ordinary messages or notifications. A person who receives a draft can still copy it or take a screenshot; Focaaa cannot remotely erase a copy they made.

Firebase, Google, and Cloudflare may process network and service-operation data under their own terms. Focaaa does not include an advertising SDK or an in-app analytics SDK in this release.

Retention and deletion

Invites expire after five minutes. Undelivered encrypted envelopes, delivery receipts, and encrypted relay media copies expire after seven days; delivered envelopes are removed when acknowledged. Expired invites, receipts, media, and rate-limit records are cleared by a daily relay job. Already downloaded encrypted media stays on a phone until its message or conversation is deleted. Unpairing revokes the active connection, deletes pending encrypted envelopes and relay media, and removes the active conversation, media, and keys from that phone. The other person can still have their own local history until they remove it. The relay deletes revoked and expired pair records after 30 days, once their delivery receipts are gone. Device registration records and push tokens remain while an installation is registered; revoking an installation clears its push token. Contact support about removal of remaining relay records.

With version 1.8.2 live drafts, the joiner's phone stores its own consented edits and the creator's phone stores received edits, including deleted text, in their encrypted local databases. They remain viewable for 24 hours and are deleted when private chat next opens or during its hourly cleanup while open. The relay does not keep a draft history. Pausing or turning sharing off stops new edits; past edits already received may remain on the creator's phone until they expire or that person removes the conversation. Unpairing removes local draft history from the phone doing the unpairing. Earlier version 1.7 could share drafts in both directions; a previously received local copy on an older phone cannot be remotely erased. Version 1.6 has no live-draft feature.

Notifications and limits

When a private message arrives while neither its full chat nor its user-opened notification-panel chat is active, Focaaa can show a generic focus reminder. Its title and text contain no contact name, message text, or conversation details. It may reveal the time of activity to someone watching your notifications. If you explicitly open Focaaa’s expanded conversation notification, it can show the selected contact and messages after unlock, and Android identifies Focaaa as the notification source. That panel stays until you swipe it away. Lock-screen display depends on Android and phone settings; tested phones hid contact and message text. Focaaa reduces casual exposure, but it cannot make the app invisible or protect against every person with access to your phone.

Your choices

You can choose your private-entry timer, set a local PIN, pause or turn off draft sharing if you joined an invite, unpair, remove a local contact, and export or restore encrypted read-only message history. Keep your device, PIN, and recovery key secure; anyone with access to an unlocked private chat may see locally stored history. Contact xlr8beast2h@gmail.com for privacy questions, access or deletion requests concerning relay records, or to raise a complaint. We will consider requests under the data protection laws that apply to you; local copies on another person's phone may be outside our control. Please do not email your PIN, invite code, recovery key, or message contents.
